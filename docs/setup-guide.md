# Setup guide

*[Español](guia-de-montaje.md)*

For one coach, in five minutes, with no Internet.

## What you need
- **Camera phone:** Android 10 or newer with the InstantReplay app; a good rear camera helps.
- **Viewer:** any phone, tablet or computer with a browser (Chrome recommended). Nothing to install.
- **Tripod** or phone mount.
- **Charger or power bank** for the Camera phone. Continuous recording is power-hungry; with a weak charger the battery can still drain while plugged in.

## Before the match
1. **Mount the phone** in **landscape**, in the shade if it is sunny (it heats up), and **plugged in**.
2. On the Camera phone, **turn on the Wi-Fi hotspot**. That is the simplest setup because it depends on no other network; a travel router also works.
3. Open **InstantReplay → Camera**. Allow camera and notifications the first time.
4. **Frame the shot** with the large preview.

## Connect the Viewer
- **From a phone:** join the Camera phone's Wi-Fi and **scan the QR** on the Camera screen with the phone camera. The Viewer opens already connected.
- **From a tablet or computer:** join that Wi-Fi, open the **address** shown on the Camera (e.g. `http://10.x.x.x:8080`) in a browser and type the **6-digit code**.

As soon as a Viewer connects, **the Camera screen goes black** to save battery and heat. It keeps recording, and a tap wakes it.

## During the match (Viewer)
- **SAVE 20 s** keeps the last 20 seconds plus 2 after the tap; **10 / 30 / 60 s** save other lengths.
- Right after, a **tag bar** shows for a few seconds. Tap one (**Good · Bad · Other**) to classify the play, or ignore it.
- The **REC dot** at the top means the Camera records fine. Problems (not recording, overheating, low battery) show as a warning; tap the dot for details.

## Time-outs and half-time
- The **clip list** shows the newest first, with a thumbnail and a "tag · time" name. Filter by tag with the chips on top.
- Tap a clip to watch it **looping full screen**: 0.25× / 0.5× / 1×, frame stepping and a scrub bar.
- On a computer or tablet you can **download** the clip.

## Tags
**⋮ → Edit tags**: add, rename, delete, reorder and recolour. Tags are stored on each Viewer.

## Warnings and problems
- **"The phone is hot"**: move it into the shade; if it continues, lower the quality under **Advanced** (720p).
- **The Viewer does not connect:** check it is on the **Camera phone's Wi-Fi**, not another network.
- **Clips work but the live view does not show:** reload the Viewer page. If it still fails, note the phone and browser you used and report it.
- **Important:** in the web Viewer, clips **only live in that tab**. **Download** the ones you want before closing it.
